# Experience and Content Design

Status: Refreshed experience reported live; functional acceptance pending  
Last updated: 27 November 2026

## Executive summary

The design turns CX SharePoint into a practical entry point for employees seeking tools, guidance and support. Rather than migrate everything into one place, it connects employee intent to authoritative resources and clear next steps.

**Employee need → CoMWeb guidance → authoritative source or service → clear next step**

## Delivered experience

Seven page exports were inspected after the reported 18 November go-live:

| Page | Employee outcome |
|---|---|
| Customer Experience | Understand the CX offer and find a starting point |
| Understand who our customers are | Find customer segments, modes and supporting resources |
| Act on customer data and feedback | Find customer insights and feedback pathways |
| Improve your service for customers | Access practical improvement guidance |
| Get training and grow your skills | Find relevant capability-building resources |
| Get help delivering customer service | Access operational guidance and support |
| Connect with the CX team | Find relevant CX contacts and engagement pathways |

## Design decisions

- Employee needs first; concise orientation rather than organisational navigation.
- One authoritative source; link rather than duplicate content.
- Multiple ways in: global search, mega menu, direct links and CoMpanion.
- Use existing SharePoint components and the minimum useful experience.
- Preserve original approved resource filenames and release grouping; review naming conventions after the final sprint.

## Post-launch improvement

Native intent-based resource filtering was demonstrated on a draft SharePoint page on 26–27 November. This is an optional enhancement to improve resource discovery independently of storage location. Multi-intent matching, employee access, usability and comparison with the library approach remain to be validated. It is not a production approval or closure dependency.

Complete functional PVT, CoMpanion source and retrieval checks, feedback triage, legacy retirement and BAU ownership during closure. The broader Customers and Communities Hub remains distinct from the seven-page CX release.
