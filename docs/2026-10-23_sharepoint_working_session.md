## Confirmed working direction

1. **Content review, not IA redesign.** Facilitation retains the six-needs structure. Stakeholders may suggest wording changes, but the sessions are not invitations to redesign navigation from scratch.
2. **Review flow.** Set context and employee-first principles; ask what employees currently seek from the stakeholder area *before* showing the draft; allow silent scanning; review sections for accuracy, missing or unnecessary content, plain English, links/resources and the description of the offer; identify future page owner and review frequency; play back gaps and changes.
3. **Test link conventions.** Ask for examples of what participants expect under **quick links / quick clicks** versus **popular tools**, and what belongs in the right-hand sidebar and 'still not sure' / 'explore by what you need' prompts. Combine into a short discussion section.
4. **Reuse Miro facilitation assets.** Mindy has drafted the discussion script, facilitator cheat sheet, shared introductory frames and group-specific draft pages. A note-taker template was still in preparation. Avoid leading questions. Participants may appear in multiple groups where they bring different perspectives.
5. **Release discipline.** Dennis described early November as a target for a good first attempt, with further time for checking links and testing. Do not treat 'two weeks' as an approved deadline; reduce scope rather than expanding work over multiple further sprints.
6. **Keep hub exploration proportionate.** First improve the core CX pages and employee-focused wording; explore the role of additional hub/journey experiences in parallel, with possible incremental evolution rather than a single big-bang launch.

## Open decisions and dependencies

- **Release:** exact first-version launch date, readiness criteria, testing period and responsible publisher remain to be confirmed. The earlier delivery plan's **14 October–3 November** window is a planning reference, not a launch approval.
- **Page count:** the meeting mentions **seven pages** as the current working set. Reconcile this against the earlier draft homepage + level-two-page counts in design artefacts before treating the number as a fixed baseline.
- **Governance:** page-level business owner, maintenance cadence, change and approval controls, and link checks still need agreement through practical delivery.
- **Links:** authoritative target locations, ownership of a link inventory and handling of broken/retired links require a working method.
- **Hub terminology and employee experience:** 'hub', 'hub template' and additional navigational layers are being used inconsistently. Jenny's health/safety/wellbeing template enquiry is not evidence of an agreed navigation model. Dennis asked to separate that specific request and clarify the wider model with Jasmine. An additional layer should not be assumed.
- **Platform:** clarify storage locations, feasible SharePoint components, metadata fields and handoff with Digital before final publication.
- **Stakeholder sessions:** pending invitations/calendar moves require checking; a verbal time suggestion is not a confirmed calendar booking.

## Strategic distinction

**Release 1.0:** make the current priority CX pages accurate, navigable, linked and owned.  
**Progressive evolution:** use governance and feedback loops to improve content connections, employee journeys and possible hubs. Do not use uncertainty about the future hub model to stall the minimum useful experience.
