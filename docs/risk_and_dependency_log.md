## Constraints

### Current CoMWeb direction

Current Digital guidance indicates the future employee experience should:

- organise around employee needs;
- use one authoritative source;
- support multiple ways in;
- link rather than duplicate;
- treat CoMWeb as a pathway rather than a repository.

### Benchmarking

External or internal examples should not be treated as implementation requirements.

Benchmarking ideas must be checked against:

- employee need;
- current CoMWeb governance;
- authoritative-source rules;
- SharePoint capability;
- accessibility;
- ownership;
- maintenance effort.
