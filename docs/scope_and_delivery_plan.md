# Scope and Delivery Plan

Status: Draft  
Current phase: Define and Develop (23 September–13 October 2026)  
Last updated: 30 September 2026

## Purpose

Define the working scope, phase outcomes, decision points and delivery boundaries for the CX SharePoint Refresh.

## Problem statement

CX information has grown across multiple internal spaces without a consistently clear purpose, structure or ownership model.

This creates uncertainty about:

- what content should live where;
- what should be maintained;
- what source is authoritative;
- how employees should find and use CX information;
- how CX should present its offer across the organisation.

## In scope

- review current purple, green and black CX pages;
- identify outdated, duplicated and fragmented content;
- understand employee needs and expectations;
- clarify the role of CX SharePoint within the broader employee information ecosystem;
- benchmark relevant internal information experiences;
- align with Digital on governance, content homes and CoMpanion implications;
- define future information architecture and navigation;
- clarify the CX offer and engagement pathways;
- define content ownership and maintenance expectations;
- prototype the future experience;
- identify the minimum implementation needed.

## Out of scope

- migrating all existing content by default;
- creating new content without a demonstrated employee need;
- duplicating authoritative information already maintained elsewhere;
- redesigning unrelated internal spaces;
- custom platform development unless a validated need emerges.

## Scoping: 2–22 September

The deck records team formation, working rhythms, problem definition and success expectations; current-state review, content gathering, CX employee interviews and employee usage analysis; understanding the CoMWeb upgrade, synthesising findings and preparing BLT alignment.

The deck reports 12 interviews and current-state findings. Completion of the full content inventory, inbound-link audit and usage analysis is not established by the deck.

## Define and Develop: 23 September–13 October

Planned activities in the deck:

- prioritise the CX product offering;
- organise content in line with the broader organisational CoMWeb direction;
- align on key CX employee experiences, tone and language;
- wireframe the CX destination;
- mock up links into broader employee journey hubs;
- develop SharePoint links to backend resources, including the proposed PDF approach, with a file structure supporting one authoritative source and governance;
- bring the work together into three employee experiences for high-level BLT agreement.

The three experiences are not specified in the deck. Their scope requires definition. The resource formats, final content homes, file structure and implementation approach remain subject to validation.

Supporting design work should resolve content inventory and classification, ownership, important inbound links, accessibility and SharePoint feasibility before implementation decisions are finalised.

## Define and Deliver 1.0: 14 October–3 November

The planned goal is to deliver the first version of the CX destination. The deck explicitly lists an internal change management plan; its remaining weekly activity slots are unfilled.

Proposed implementation tasks for planning, rather than confirmed deck commitments:

- build agreed pages and navigation;
- update, consolidate, link, archive or retire approved content;
- preserve or redirect important inbound links;
- publish reviewed content;
- verify access, links and accessibility;
- establish ownership and maintenance controls.

## Governance and maintenance: 4–24 November

The deck plans a transition into governance and maintenance cycles. Accountable owners, review cadence, publishing controls and retirement rules still need agreement.

## Key decision points

- role of CX content within CoMWeb and Digital Dashboard;
- primary employee needs to design around;
- authoritative source for overlapping content;
- content to keep, update, remove, archive or link;
- future information architecture;
- CX offering model;
- prototype sign-off;
- implementation feasibility;
- ownership and maintenance model.
