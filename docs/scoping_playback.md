# Scoping Playback

Status: Draft  
Last updated: 30 September 2026

## Playback purpose

Confirm the proposed direction for the CX SharePoint Refresh and support progression through Define and Develop.

The playback should validate:

- the problem being solved;
- current-state evidence;
- employee research;
- Digital direction;
- scope and design principles;
- Define and Develop approach;
- decisions required.

It is not a detailed page-design review.

## What the evidence now says

### Current state

- much content is outdated;
- overlap creates uncertainty;
- useful content is hard to find.

### Benchmarking

- many spaces are team-first;
- navigation often mirrors organisational structure;
- access is often restricted by default.

### Employee research

Twelve interviews produced five key insights:

1. employees are needs-led, not browse-led;
2. the CX offer is unclear;
3. trust matters as much as findability;
4. employees value useful resources and clear next steps;
5. different needs require different experiences.

### Digital direction

The broader employee experience should:

- organise around employee needs;
- use one authoritative source;
- support multiple ways in.

## Emerging future direction

### Opportunity 1

Clarify how CX can help.

### Opportunity 2

Make tools and resources easier to access.

### Opportunity 3

Make trusted information easier to identify.

## Emerging content model

CoMWeb becomes a pathway, not a repository.

Employee need  
→ CoMWeb guidance  
→ authoritative source or service  
→ clear next step

## Content homes

### CoMWeb / purple

Organisation-facing, broad-audience, concise, task-focused and CoMpanion-friendly.

### Digital Dashboard / green

Specialist, role-specific, branch / team, collaborative or permissioned content.

### Rule

Link, do not duplicate.

## Decisions required

1. Confirm the needs-led direction.
2. Confirm simplification over migration.
3. Confirm the one-authoritative-source principle.
4. Confirm the working split between CoMWeb and Digital Dashboard.
5. Confirm the Define and Develop approach.

## Next value drop

By 13 October 2026, return with:

- implementation recommendation.
