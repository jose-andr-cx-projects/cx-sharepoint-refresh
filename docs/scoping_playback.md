# Stakeholder Playbacks

Status: Working engagement summary; delivery evidence and planned activities distinguished  
Last updated: 27 November 2026

## Executive summary

Stakeholder engagement has been embedded throughout the CX SharePoint Refresh, from understanding employee needs and aligning leadership on the problem, through collaborative design, Digital alignment, implementation and post-launch feedback. The approach has been to share evidence and tangible outputs at meaningful decision points rather than rely on a single end-of-project presentation.

The recorded engagement includes 12 CX employee interviews, two BLT presentations and an initial Director's weekly awareness message, supplemented by internal design working sessions and CoMWeb alignment. Following reported go-live on 18 November, an employee feedback survey provided six responses and seven proposed improvement actions. Together these activities informed the needs-led experience and established a mechanism for continued improvement; they do not demonstrate organisation-wide adoption or formal acceptance.

## How engagement influenced delivery

- Employee interviews shifted the emphasis from branch structure to employee needs, useful resources and clear next steps.
- Leadership and Digital discussions reinforced simplification, authoritative sources and multiple ways into CX information.
- Design working sessions supported iterative refinement of the employee-facing structure and practical platform testing.
- Post-launch feedback provides a route to prioritise corrections and improvement opportunities rather than treating publication as the end of engagement.

## Historical scoping playback — October 2026

The original scoping playback below is retained as evidence of preparation and proposed decisions, not a record of formal approval.

### Playback purpose

Confirm the proposed direction for the CX SharePoint Refresh and support progression through Define and Develop.

The playback should validate:

- the problem being solved;
- current-state evidence;
- employee research;
- Digital direction;
- scope and design principles;
- Define and Develop approach;
- decisions required.

It is not a detailed page-design review.

### What the evidence now says

#### Current state

- much content is outdated;
- overlap creates uncertainty;
- useful content is hard to find.

#### Benchmarking

- many spaces are team-first;
- navigation often mirrors organisational structure;
- access is often restricted by default.

#### Employee research

Twelve interviews produced five key insights:

1. employees are needs-led, not browse-led;
2. the CX offer is unclear;
3. trust matters as much as findability;
4. employees value useful resources and clear next steps;
5. different needs require different experiences.

#### Digital direction

The broader employee experience should:

- organise around employee needs;
- use one authoritative source;
- support multiple ways in.

### Emerging future direction

#### Opportunity 1

Clarify how CX can help.

#### Opportunity 2

Make tools and resources easier to access.

#### Opportunity 3

Make trusted information easier to identify.

### Emerging content model

CoMWeb becomes a pathway, not a repository.

Employee need  
→ CoMWeb guidance  
→ authoritative source or service  
→ clear next step

### Content homes

#### CoMWeb / purple

Organisation-facing, broad-audience, concise, task-focused and CoMpanion-friendly.

#### Digital Dashboard / green

Specialist, role-specific, branch / team, collaborative or permissioned content.

#### Rule

Link, do not duplicate.

### Decisions required

1. Confirm the needs-led direction.
2. Confirm simplification over migration.
3. Confirm the one-authoritative-source principle.
4. Confirm the working split between CoMWeb and Digital Dashboard.
5. Confirm the Define and Develop approach.
