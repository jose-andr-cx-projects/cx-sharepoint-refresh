## Key decisions and risks

- Preserve authoritative sources and original approved resource filenames; naming convention review deferred until after the final sprint (D-019/D-020).
- Legacy pages retained for up to three weeks after go-live; retirement and CoMpanion exclusion require verification (D-017).
- Publishing SOP, ongoing ownership and release acceptance remain open. Resource curation feasibility is not a closure dependency.
