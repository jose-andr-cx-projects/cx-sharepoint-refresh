# Experience and Content Design

Status: Draft  
Current phase: Define and Develop (23 September–13 October 2026)  
Last updated: 30 September 2026

## Purpose

Define the future CX employee experience as discovery matures.

## Design intent

The future experience should help employees:

1. understand what CX can help them with;
2. find relevant tools, services, information or guidance;
3. identify trusted and authoritative information;
4. know the next step;
5. reach the right source, service or specialist content.

## Working experience model

The emerging model is:

**Employee need → CoMWeb guidance → authoritative source or service → clear next step**

CoMWeb should guide, orient and connect employees rather than hold everything itself.

## Experience principles

### Employee needs first

Organise around what employees are trying to understand, find or do rather than around branch ownership.

### One authoritative source

Provide enough context to help employees understand what they need, then connect them to the right source instead of duplicating content.

### Multiple ways in

Support navigation, search, CoMpanion and direct links.

### Lead with the CX offer

Make it clear what employees can come to CX for and what they can do next.

### Design for trust

Make currency, ownership and authoritative source clear.

### Use the minimum useful experience

Do not create new pages by default. Prefer updating, consolidating, linking or surfacing before creating net-new content.

## Emerging employee-need frame

A current CoMWeb recommendation organises content around broad employee needs rather than branch structure.

For CX and related support, the emerging employee need is:

> How do I understand, engage and respond to customers and communities?

This is a working direction and should be treated as current guidance rather than a finalised information architecture.

## Resource hub direction

The current CoMWeb model suggests specialist needs can sit within resource hubs.

For CX, an emerging `Customers and Communities` hub could orient employees to needs such as:

- understand customer and community insights;
- engage customers and communities;
- improve customer communication and experiences;
- build capability;
- manage customer feedback and complaints.

The final scope and structure require validation.

## Information architecture criteria

Each category should:

- represent a meaningful employee need;
- use understandable employee language;
- avoid duplicating authoritative content;
- be distinguishable from other categories;
- support multiple discovery pathways;
- have clear ownership;
- be maintainable over time.

## Content-home model

### CoMWeb / purple

Use for:

- broad employee audience;
- concise and task-focused content;
- guidance, tools and services;
- scannable and accessible information;
- content structured for CoMpanion / AI search.

### Digital Dashboard / green

Use for:

- role-specific information;
- branch or team resources;
- working material;
- collaborative content;
- document libraries;
- permissioned content.

### Rule

**Link, do not duplicate.**

A joined-up employee experience does not require everything to live in the same system.

## Benchmarking constraint

Benchmarking should inform ideas, not dictate implementation.

Before adopting a pattern, test:

1. what employee need it addresses;
2. whether it aligns with current CoMWeb governance direction;
3. whether an authoritative source already exists;
4. whether the pattern is feasible in SharePoint;
5. whether it supports accessibility;
6. who would own and maintain it;
7. whether it reduces or adds complexity.

Working rule:

**Benchmark the intent, not necessarily the implementation.**

## Prototype scenarios

Use realistic employee tasks, including:

- I need CX help but do not know which capability I need.
- I want to find a CX tool or resource.
- I need customer or community insight.
- I need support to improve a customer experience.
- I need to understand what happens next or who to contact.
- I need to reach an authoritative source or approved service pathway.
